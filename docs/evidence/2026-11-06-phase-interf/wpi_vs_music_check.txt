VERDICT
  [PASS] one emitter, 0.5 deg requirement: WPI 0.147 deg vs CRB 0.121 deg
         (1.22x the floor). ISSM 0.362 deg, narrowband MUSIC 0.708 deg.
         MUSIC cannot beat the bound, so it cannot improve single-emitter
         accuracy at all -- the floor is the bandwidth, not the estimator.
  [PASS] two emitters: the shipped 2x2 spans rank 2 at one elevation,
         so NO subspace method can represent two emitters on it --
         2D MUSIC included, at any SNR. A 3x3 spans rank 3 and does
         resolve them. Multi-emitter capability is an array change,
         not an estimator change.
  [PASS] calibration: one coefficient per channel degrades from 0.13 deg
         to 0.50 deg as residual delay grows; per-bin calibration stays
         at 0.15 deg. A 2 GHz band needs frequency-dependent calibration,
         and no estimator fixes a calibration error it does not model.
  [INFO] geometry: 0.5 mm of element position error alone costs 1.11 deg,
         i.e. more than the simulated 0.5 deg requirement itself.

  So the answer to 'MUSIC for 0.5 deg' is no: 0.5 deg is bounded by
  bandwidth (met statistically at 0.15 deg), by calibration and by
  geometry, and MUSIC trades none of those. Its one genuine benefit --
  two-emitter resolution -- needs a different array: the shipped 2x2
  spans rank 2 at one elevation and cannot represent two emitters at
  any SNR.
