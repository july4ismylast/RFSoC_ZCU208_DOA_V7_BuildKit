VERDICT
  Modulation is a MODE, not a value. It does not drift, it does not
  jitter: it *is* a class, and it changes only when the emitter
  reconfigures. So the rule of V07 17 applies with a different error:
  the error of a label is a probability, not a degree.
   - the label is right 100% of frames at 20 dB, 100% at 10 dB and
     20% at 0 dB -- which is chance for five classes.
   - a vote cannot fix a biased classifier, only a random one: at 5 dB
     it moves the accuracy 70% -> 77% and the flips 0.26 -> 0.18 per
     frame. It buys stability, not truth.
   - two emitters that share direction and bandwidth and differ only in
     modulation are separated at 20 dB (covered 3.00 of 3 with the label,
     1.42 without) and at 10 dB (3.00 against 1.42), and not at 0 dB
     (1.92 with against 1.67 without).
   - two emitters with the SAME modulation are never separated by AMC:
     covered 1.42 of 3 with the label at 20 dB against 1.58 without.
  So AMC enters the cost as a MODE term with a vote, after direction and
  bandwidth, and only where they fail. It is an identity aid, not an
  authenticator: an emitter that switches modulation switches its own
  label, and a decoy that copies the waveform copies the label with it.
